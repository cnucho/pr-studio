# KG Statistics Production System — Screenshot Selection Plan

## Communication job

By the end, Kyrgyzstan National Statistical Committee stakeholders should
understand that the system supports the complete survey-production sequence,
see how an operator uses each major stage, and understand how guidance,
updates, and SaaS support reduce operational risk.

## Audience-facing sequence

1. Survey project and controlled access
2. Questionnaire design
3. Independent questionnaire approval
4. Collection preparation
5. Offline collection and synchronization
6. Cleaning review
7. Analysis, indicators, and tables
8. Independent verification
9. Publication approval
10. Immutable HTML, PDF, CSV, and XLSX release
11. In-application help and learning
12. Maintenance, updates, and SaaS support

## Candidate-production rule

Create three or four candidates for every major stage before choosing any
presentation image:

- the operator's starting or input screen;
- contextual Help opened deliberately by the operator;
- the accepted receipt or active evidence after completion;
- where useful, a second evidence view such as access, lineage, or release
  inventory.

The target is 30–36 raw candidates for 12–15 selected presentation images.

## Selection criteria

Each selected image must:

1. prove a distinct survey-stage capability;
2. show the real authenticated React operator application;
3. remain legible when placed on a 16:9 English presentation slide;
4. avoid passwords, tokens, respondent rows, and local filesystem paths;
5. avoid duplicating another selected image's message;
6. show either an operator decision, an accepted evidence result, or usable
   guidance.

## Video shortlist rule

Only interactions that are materially clearer in motion will become videos:

- creating and opening a controlled project;
- editing and submitting a questionnaire;
- synchronizing an accepted field batch;
- editing indicators and producing a report;
- recording independent verification;
- approving and generating the immutable release;
- downloading representative official formats.

Static metadata, policy explanations, help content, and maintenance guidance
remain screenshot-led.
